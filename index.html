Privacy Policy for Dukaan Stock

This Privacy Policy explains how Dukaan Stock (package ID: com.aistudio.dukaanstock.vmrkqz), developed and operated by Pranjal Chandrakar ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Dukaan Stock Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide billing, inventory, khata ledger, and cloud-sync functionality:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In (via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), we receive your Google account name, email address, and a unique Firebase User ID (UID) to authenticate you and isolate your business workspace.
  • Business & Financial Records You Enter: You may voluntarily enter and store business details (business name, phone, email, GSTIN, address, invoice settings), customer and supplier records (party names, phone numbers, emails, GSTINs, billing/shipping addresses, ledger balances), inventory catalog data (item names, HSN/SAC codes, prices, tax rates, stock counts), cash/bank/UPI account labels, and invoices, purchases, or expense transactions.
  • On-Device App Preferences: Theme preferences (such as Light, Dark, or System mode) are stored locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.

2. Permissions Used

Dukaan Stock follows least-privilege access and requests only network permissions required for authentication and cloud synchronization:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used solely for Google Sign-In and syncing your business data with Cloud Firestore.

The app does not access your device contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, call logs, or external media storage.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account securely using Google Sign-In.
  • To store, sync, and back up your inventory, invoices, party ledgers, and reports across sessions.
  • To calculate GST totals, stock levels, balances, and business summaries inside the app.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties, and the app contains no third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Storage

Dukaan Stock relies on Google Firebase services to operate reliably:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication — For secure user login (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Cloud Firestore — For storing and synchronizing your workspace records per user ID (Firebase Privacy & Security).

5. Data Security

All communication between the app and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to Cloud Firestore records is restricted by authenticated security rules so that only your signed-in Google account can read or modify your business workspace.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your business records remain stored in your private workspace as long as you maintain your account. You can edit or delete parties, inventory items, and bank/cash accounts directly inside the app at any time.

Requesting Complete Account & Data Deletion: To request permanent deletion of your Dukaan Stock account and all associated Cloud Firestore data, please email pranjalchandrakar222@gmail.com from your registered Google email address with the subject line "Dukaan Stock Account Deletion Request". We will verify ownership and permanently delete your account and cloud records within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Dukaan Stock is a business management utility and is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
